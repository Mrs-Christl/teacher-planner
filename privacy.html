Privacy Policy

Teacher Planner by Mrs Christl · Last updated 21 November 2026

Teacher Planner is a lesson-planning tool for teachers. This page explains what information it uses and where that information is kept.

What the planner stores

The planner stores only what you type into it: your classes, timetable, term dates, lesson topics, homework, notes, checklists, assessment dates and settings. It is designed so that no student names are entered. Students are recorded only as codes or initials that you choose.

Where your information is kept

When you sign in with Google, your planner is saved in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. This folder is hidden from your normal Drive files and can only be used by this planner. A copy is also kept in your web browser on each device you use, so the planner works without an internet connection.

Your planner is not sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or person. The planner's author cannot see your planner. Other people who use the planner each have their own separate planner in their own Google Drive.

Google account access

The planner asks Google for one permission only: to see, create and delete its own data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files, emails or contacts.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

You can sign out at any time in Settings → Backup & New Year, which removes the planner from that device. To delete your planner from Google Drive, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find this planner and choose “Delete hidden app data”. You can also remove the planner's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact Mrs Christl using the email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
